## Level 1 deliverables and timing
One original vertical video approximately 20–45 seconds plus three high-resolution photos or clean clips.
Production deadline: five calendar days after correct fit is confirmed following receipt; resolve replacements first.
Review target: within 24 hours; one reasonable correction for product accuracy, factual errors, disclosure or missed requirements. Upload is not approval.
Publish approved content on the agreed account/platform by the specified creator-local date/time and keep it public for the 15-day trial, except safety, platform enforcement or legal compliance removal with prompt notification.
Provide the organic link, code, offer and CTA after approval and before publication.
Disclose both gifted products and potential affiliate commission.
No minimum sales requirement; no return/reimbursement of the gift solely for lack of sales.

## Tracking, offer and money
Level 1 links, codes and audience offer run for 15 days from publication.
Each customer's Level 1 attribution window lasts 15 days from their first valid click while the link is active. Later clicks do not reset it. Eligible purchases can occur after the trial ends. Expiry blocks new qualifying clicks, not existing valid windows.
Level 2 activation template specifies 30 days after each qualified click; final accepted Level 2 terms control. Level 3 window remains to be specified.
Qualifying Net Sales are product revenue received after discounts, excluding tax, shipping, cancellations, returns, refunds, chargebacks, fraud, creator self-purchases and free-product retail value.
Customer offer: qualifying women's velvet top, bottom and T-shirt set includes one complimentary dog velvet shirt, subject to stock, sizing and offer/return terms.
Commissions remain pending through applicable cancellation/return validation. Payment method, payment deadline and exact hold duration are placeholders; do not invent them.
Day-15 measurement is provisional. Validate later orders on rolling holds and share aggregate results without customer personal data.

## Organic and paid content licenses
Creator retains ownership.
Organic license: worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free approved content and approved embodied name/handle/image/voice/likeness on MeeDoo-owned digital channels, for 12 months following content delivery.
Permitted adaptations include crop, resize, captions, translation, sound, product information and CTA, without materially misrepresenting opinion or experience. Other identifiable participants need permission.
Paid license: approved content through MeeDoo-controlled advertising accounts, for 90 consecutive days from first paid impression. First paid use must begin within six months of content delivery; notify creator beforehand. If not activated in time, it expires.
The source includes these limited paid rights in the gift/commission compensation; a separate usage fee is not due within that accepted scope.
Creator-account ads, Meta Partnership Ads, Spark Ads, whitelisting, broader rights and extensions require separate agreements/authorization. These rights must be explicitly included in the accepted version; never infer them for legacy collaborations.
Store content delivery, notice and first paid impression dates to evaluate license availability.

## Traffic separation and integrity
Separate creator-organic URLs per creator/platform/post from paid-media URLs per advertisement.
Use creator_organic versus paid_social medium values and retain content/creator/ad identifiers.
Do not use creator affiliate links/codes in MeeDoo-paid ads.
The source credits the last qualified organic click or approved organic code, and paid media when its qualified click is last. Direct/unknown traffic does not overwrite a qualified source.
Resolve code-versus-last-paid-click conflicts explicitly before implementing attribution; code-only timing is not fully specified.
Prohibit artificial or fraudulent engagement, traffic and transactions while allowing genuine interactions and approved promotions.
